**Note on item 8:** the LightGBM mean reported in the manuscript text for ridge (`T.cov_raw.mean()`/`T.cov_std.mean()`, i.e. the mean over all 20 transitions including the 2 controls) differs slightly from the 18-non-control mean used elsewhere in the paper for other statistics — this is a real inconsistency in which denominator (20 vs 18 transitions) is used across different sentences in the manuscript, and should be checked and made consistent before submission.

## Explicit assessment of the six named capabilities

- **Cross-pipeline prediction instability:** demonstrated directly — `MAE_raw` in `transitions_main.csv` rises from ~2.5–3.0 years (source) to as much as 18.5 years (CIVET targets). **Solidly shown.**
- **Conformal prediction coverage degradation:** demonstrated directly and is the paper's core, best-supported finding. **Solidly shown.**
- **Failure of weighted conformal prediction:** demonstrated for the specific logistic-regression-density-ratio implementation used here. **Shown, but scoped to this implementation** — do not generalize to "weighted CP can never work for pipeline shift" without qualification.
- **Generalizability beyond ABIDE I:** **not demonstrated.** No second dataset was analyzed. This is the single largest gap in the evidence relative to any broad claim of generalizability, and both research-report passes independently flagged it (OASIS-3 access and multi-pipeline derivatives were investigated and found unresolved/unavailable — see `Literature_Review/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md` and the project's earlier OASIS-3 access-route findings).
- **Clinical relevance:** **not demonstrated and not claimed as a strong result.** ABIDE I is pediatric/young-adult and ASD-enriched; the manuscript's own age-stratified results show the model already fails in the sparse older-adult tail even at the source pipeline, which argues against extrapolating to a clinical/dementia population without new data.
- **A novel empirical contribution:** **reasonably supported** — the measurement (coverage loss under a same-scan pipeline swap, with an exhaustive 20-transition design, a genuine negative control, and several named remedies compared on the same data) was not found elsewhere in two independent literature passes. The paper does not claim a novel conformal *method* — correctly, since the ReForm-style recalibration is an approximation of prior work, not a new method.
